Quickstart Rules (60-Second Guide)

🎯 The Objective

Deliver client projects and complete office milestones. The first agency to reach 10 Kudos (⭐) wins immediately.

🎨 The 3 Skills (Must Match Contract Tags!)

Projects demand specific backgrounds. You cannot send two Designers to fix a database!

  • 🎨 Design (Frontend): Creative & UI interfaces.
  • ⚙️ Systems (Backend): Databases, server pipelines, architecture.
  • 🔒 Logic (Security/QA): Testing, auth gateways, code stability.

☕ Exhaustion & Coffee

When staff members ship a project, they get Exhausted (dimmed). They cannot work on another project this turn unless you click "☕ Drink Coffee" to stand them back up!

⚠️ Project Penalties

If your Team Power + Roll (1 to 4) fails to beat the project difficulty, you trigger the penalty box (losing coffee, energy, or burning out staff on Super Projects)!
